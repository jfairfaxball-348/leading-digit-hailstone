# Prior-Art Audit Protocol

The audit asks whether the frozen system is already known exactly or is contained essentially for free in a known general class.

Search layers:

1. Exact formula and notation variants for `3n+2L(n)+1`.
2. Generalized `an+b` and piecewise affine Collatz maps.
3. Residue-class-dependent Collatz maps.
4. Digit-dependent arithmetic dynamical systems, especially rules depending on first/leading digit.
5. Digit-sum, digit-product, and digit-reversal iterations where the construction may subsume a leading-digit correction.
6. Leading-digit and Benford-law studies of ordinary Collatz trajectories, separating observational digit statistics from digit-dependent dynamics.
7. Computational repositories and sequence databases where equivalent maps may appear without a conventional paper.

For every candidate source record: bibliographic identity, exact rule/class, domain, equivalence argument or reason it is only analogous, known theorems/computation, and relevance to novelty. Do not infer novelty from failure of literal-text search.
